## Howland 1.0.0

Chat, artifacts, and code on your own hardware. Free and private.

**Howland Server** runs your models and your library.
**Howland Desktop** is the app you work in.

Downloads for every platform that has a published build are on the
[project page](https://github.com/ayers-software-repair/howland-releases#readme),
which is generated from this release and so always matches what is actually here.

Builds that need a signing certificate are published as those certificates land; a platform
missing from the list above is not yet signed, rather than not yet built.

Report an issue: https://github.com/ayers-software-repair/howland-releases/issues
