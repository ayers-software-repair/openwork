## Howland 1.0.0

Cowork, artifacts, and code on your own hardware. Free and private.

**Howland Server** runs your models and your library. Mac is complete; the Windows and Linux
servers are early previews.

**Howland Desktop** is the app you work in: Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), Windows, and Linux.

**Howland Mobile** arrives when our app store accounts are approved.

The installers are not signed yet, so expect a warning on first launch.
Something broken? Tell us: https://github.com/ayers-software-repair/howland-releases/issues
